# OIBSIP Data Analytics Internship

## Task 1: Exploratory Data Analysis on Retail Sales Data

### Project Overview

This project focuses on performing Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
on a retail sales dataset to understand sales performance,
product performance, customer segments, regional trends and
profitability.

### Objective

The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyze monthly and quarterly sales trends
- Analyze customer segments
- Identify top-performing products
- Analyze revenue by product category
- Analyze profitability
- Study regional sales performance
- Identify relationships between numerical variables
- Generate meaningful business insights
- Provide actionable business recommendations

### Tools and Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Google Colab
- GitHub

### Dataset

The analysis was performed using a SuperStore retail sales dataset.

The dataset contains information related to:

- Orders
- Customers
- Products
- Categories
- Regions
- Sales
- Quantity
- Discount
- Profit
- Shipping Cost

### Analysis Performed

1. Dataset inspection
2. Data quality checking
3. Data cleaning
4. Descriptive statistics
5. Monthly sales trend
6. Quarterly sales trend
7. Numerical variable analysis
8. Category-wise sales analysis
9. Top 10 products analysis
10. Customer segment analysis
11. Correlation analysis
12. Profitability analysis
13. Business insights
14. Business recommendations

### Key Business Results

- Total Sales: 12,642,905
- Total Profit: 1,469,034.82
- Profit Margin: 11.62%
- Total Quantity Sold: 178,312
- Top Sales Category: Technology
- Top Product: Apple Smart Phone, Full Size
- Top Sales Region: Central
- Most Profitable Category: Technology

### Business Recommendations

1. Focus on the Technology category because it is the highest
   performing category in terms of sales and profit.

2. Promote high-performing products through targeted marketing
   campaigns and product bundles.

3. Strengthen the Central region through customer retention
   strategies and targeted promotions.

4. Review discounts, product costs and shipping costs to improve
   overall profitability.

5. Use sales trends and product performance for better inventory
   planning.

### Conclusion

The analysis provided valuable insights into retail sales,
product performance, customer segments, regional sales and
profitability.

The findings can support better marketing, inventory planning,
regional strategies and profitability improvement.
